04 / About

I'm with you from the first sketch to launch day, and long after the site goes live.

I came to software from geology, so I'm used to messy data and long field days.

I like being involved in both the build and the planning. I'll work out what you need, explain the trade-offs and keep you updated while I build.

I've written code, reviewed other people's code, tested sites before launch and managed projects from start to finish. Having done each part myself, I usually see a problem coming, like a vague brief or a page that breaks on mobile, and sort it out before it turns into a delay.
