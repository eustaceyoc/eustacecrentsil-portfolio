A / Service

Website & Web Application Development

I build first websites and replace ones that no longer fit. I also build custom apps, portals and online stores for teams that need more than a brochure site.

You might need this if...

  • No website, or one that doesn't explain what you do or bring in enquiries.
  • A site you can't update without calling a developer.
  • Work spread across spreadsheets and messages that needs a custom app.
  • An online store or booking flow that's clumsy on a phone.

What I can do

  • Business websites and landing pages
  • E-commerce stores
  • Custom web applications and client or member portals
  • WordPress sites, themes and plugin integrations (for example LMS and certificates)
  • UI/UX design: mobile-first layouts and clear page structure
  • Hosting and cloud setup: domains, email, AWS, cPanel or Vercel, backups
  • Search-friendly pages and analytics basics
  • Maintenance, updates and small fixes after launch

What you get

  • A site or app that works on phones and desktops
  • Content set up and checked
  • Hosting, domain and analytics in place

Technical Project Management

I help businesses with technical projects that involve several people or vendors. If you're starting with an idea, I can turn it into a brief a developer can build from.

You might need this if...

  • No timeline, or one nobody trusts.
  • Developers, designers and the client working from different pictures of the project.
  • Problems found on launch day because testing was left too late.

What I can do

  • Regular updates to you and the team
  • Quality assurance and testing before launch

What you get

  • Regular status updates
  • A QA checklist and test results before launch

Process

How a project starts

  1. Brief

    You send a few lines about the business and what you need, through the form, email or WhatsApp.

  2. Call

    We talk about who will use it, what it needs to do and any limits. This is where I ask questions.

  3. Scope and plan

    I write down what's included and what isn't, with a timeline we can agree on.

  4. Build, test, launch

    I'll share progress as I build, test before launch and help with support afterwards.
