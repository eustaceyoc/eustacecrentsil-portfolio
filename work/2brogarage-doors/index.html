01 / The business

Honest pricing, quick response

2BroGarage Doors LLC has served Charlotte, North Carolina, and the surrounding areas since 2021. It handles broken torsion springs, openers, off-track and noisy doors and full system issues, and it promises honest pricing, quality workmanship and quick response times. Neighbours name the owner, Joshua, in their reviews.

It has 214 faves on Nextdoor, where it was a Neighborhood Fave in 2024 and 2025, and a 5.0 rating on Angi from 5 reviews.

Sources: the business's Nextdoor page and Angi profile, checked October 2026.

02 / The problem

Lots of goodwill, no front door

214 Nextdoor faves is a lot of goodwill for a business that started in 2021, and the owner posts useful advice there, like the warning signs of a failing spring. Without a website, none of that is easy to find from a search. I wanted a page that's as direct as the business: what we fix, the signs to watch for, and a number to call or text.

03 / Approach

Like a hand-pasted poster

No photos at all. Instead, huge condensed type, black and safety orange on cream, and a strip of services that scrolls past like tape. The headline says what to do, "Door stuck? Call or text.", and the first section is titled "Fix it. Don't guess."

Palette

Safety orange for the call buttons and highlights, near-black for type and dark sections, and a warm cream for the page. A grey is used only for small notes.

Type

Headlines are set in Anton, a tall, condensed poster face, very large and in capitals. Body text is IBM Plex Sans, which feels practical and a little technical.

DOOR STUCK? CALL OR TEXT.

Text · IBM Plex Sans

Broken torsion springs, openers, off-track doors, noisy doors and full system issues.

Layout

  • Hero. The headline in huge type, the phone number as a big orange button, and a strip of services that scrolls sideways.
  • Jobs. "Fix it. Don't guess." The three jobs people call about first: springs, openers and off-track doors.
  • Warning signs. A dark section with the spring warning signs, paraphrased from the owner's own Nextdoor post.
  • Proof. "Proof, not promises." Neighbours' reviews with initials, next to the numbers: faves, awards, Angi rating and the year it started.
  • Area and contact. Charlotte and around, then a call-back form that asks what the door is doing.

Interactions

The only motion is the services strip in the hero, which scrolls slowly sideways like a roll of tape. On phones, "Call" and "Message" buttons stay fixed to the bottom of the screen.

Mobile

Below 900 px the sections stack into one column and the type scales down, but the headline still fills most of the screen.

04 / Build

One small file, hosted on Vercel

The site is a single static HTML file with its CSS and a little plain JavaScript inside it. No framework and no build step. It's hosted on Vercel.

  • Light. The HTML file is about 16 KB, and it uses two font families from Google Fonts.
  • Accessible. Real headings and landmarks, strong colour contrast and labels on every form field.

Safeguards

Because this is a sample site for a real business that didn't ask for it, I put a few things in place:

  • A banner at the top and a line in the footer say it is a sample site, not affiliated with or published by the business.
  • It's hidden from search engines with a noindex meta tag.
  • The form is a demo. It sends nothing and says so.
  • Every fact comes from the business's public listings, and the page says where each one came from.
  • No photos or logos are used.
