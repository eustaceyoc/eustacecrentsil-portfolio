A marketplace that connects clients with verified freelancers

01 / Overview

Background

TTG Staffing brings clients and freelancers together in one place. A client posts a job, verified freelancers bid on it, and the client picks one of them to do the work.

02 / The problem

What they needed

TTG had no website. Without one, there was no shared place for clients and freelancers to find each other, so matching people to work depended on the TTG team doing it by hand.

03 / Objectives

What the project had to do

  • Give clients a simple way to post a job.
  • Let verified freelancers see jobs and bid on them.
  • Let the client compare bids and hire one freelancer.
  • Move the matching onto the site so the team spends less time pairing people.
04 / My role

What I did

I worked on this through Clottware, which I co-founded and ran from October 2023 to December 2025. Here's what I handled.

  • I interviewed the client to work out what the site needed to do.
  • I agreed the scope and timeline with the client and sent progress updates.
  • I helped design the site and built parts of the front end.
  • I added the site's content.
  • I tested the site before launch.
  • I handled support after launch and stayed the client's main contact.
05 / Approach

Approach and key decisions

A marketplace has two kinds of users who want different things, so I treated it as one flow seen from two sides. In the requirements interviews I mapped the steps for each side (post, bid, review, hire) before anything was designed, and used that flow to scope the work and set the timeline.

The "verified" part mattered to the client: clients need to trust who is bidding. That shaped the build, because freelancers had to be checked before they could take part, rather than anyone being able to bid.

Because the core is structured data (jobs, bids and accounts), the site was built with PHP and MySQL rather than a page builder.

06 / Technologies

What it is built with

  • HTML/CSS for the interface.
  • PHP for the marketplace logic: posting, bidding and hiring.
  • MySQL to store jobs, bids and accounts.
07 / Implementation

What the site includes

The site is built around the marketplace flow:

  • Clients post a job.
  • Verified freelancers bid on it.
  • The client reviews the bids and hires one freelancer.

Before launch I tested the flow end to end from both sides, then stayed on as the client's point of contact for support.

08 / Deliverables

What was handed over

  • A marketplace web app covering job posting, bidding and hiring
  • Content set up across the site
  • Pre-launch QA of the full flow
  • Ongoing support after launch
09 / Outcomes

After launch

After launch, TTG saw more client and freelancer sign-ups, enquiries and sales. Clients can post jobs and compare bids on the site. That means the team spends less time matching people by hand.
