01 / The business

A family crew, out early six days a week

Exceptional Lawn Care is a family business in Fort Worth, Texas, run by Mario and his crew. They do regular mowing, edging and trimming, flower beds and new plantings, and bigger jobs like yard and leaf cleanups, hardscape work and sprinkler repair. They work Monday to Saturday from 7:30 am and are closed on Sundays.

Neighbours on Nextdoor named it a Neighborhood Favorite five years in a row, from 2021 to 2025, and the business describes its know-how as passed down from Mario's dad.

Sources: the business's public Nextdoor page, checked October 2026.

02 / The problem

Five years of awards, and nowhere to send people

Five Neighborhood Favorite awards in a row is a strong signal, but I couldn't find a website for the business. Someone who hears about Mario from a neighbour has to dig through Nextdoor to find the phone number, the hours and what the crew actually does.

I wanted a page that puts the phone number first, lists the work clearly and makes asking for an estimate easy, using only what the business has already made public.

03 / Approach

Put the call button first

I put a lawn photo behind the headline and made the call button prominent. On phones, a fixed bar keeps calling and requesting an estimate within reach.

A shared trade-site structure

I researched local trade websites before building these samples. Exceptional Lawn Care, Clear Water Pool Service, Jaylee Electric and Alloyd Garage Door share a layout with a large hero photo, reviews or credentials, a short process section and a prominent phone number. I changed the colours, photos and copy for each business.

Palette

A near-black forest green for the hero and dark sections, with a bright lime for buttons and highlights. A deeper green carries accents on light backgrounds, and a warm off-white sits behind the service cards.

Type

I used bold Bricolage Grotesque for headings and Inter for body text. Inter stays readable at small sizes on phones.

Mowing, edging, trimming, flower beds, new plantings and cleanups from Mario and his crew.

Layout

  • Hero. A photo of a lawn being mowed, the headline, call and estimate buttons, then a strip of three trust signals: the Nextdoor awards, family-owned, and the hours.
  • Services. Three cards (lawn service, beds and planting, cleanups and projects), each with a short list of the jobs inside it.
  • How it works. Three numbered steps: call or message, get an estimate, enjoy the yard.
  • Reviews. Short excerpts of neighbours' Nextdoor recommendations, with initials, area and month, next to a tile for the five awards.
  • Contact. A dark band with the phone number in large type, then hours, service area, a short story and an estimate form.

Interactions

Sections fade up gently as they come into view, and the hero photo zooms very slowly. If your device is set to reduce motion, both are switched off.

Mobile

Below 760 px the layout drops to one column, and a bar fixed to the bottom of the screen keeps two buttons in reach, "Call now" and "Estimate". Calling is one tap from anywhere on the page.

04 / Build

One small file, hosted on Vercel

I built this as one HTML file with CSS and a little JavaScript. There's no framework or build step. It runs on Vercel.

  • The build is small. The HTML file is about 32 KB, and it uses two font families from Google Fonts.
  • I added a skip link, headings, page landmarks and labelled form fields. Animations respect reduced-motion settings.

Safeguards

I made this as a sample, so I added these safeguards.

  • A banner at the top and a line in the footer say it is a sample site, not affiliated with or published by the business.
  • It's hidden from search engines with a noindex meta tag.
  • The form is a demo. It sends nothing and says so.
  • Every fact comes from the business's public listings, and the page says where each one came from.
  • Photos are from Unsplash, credited in the footer. They are stock images, not the crew's own work.
