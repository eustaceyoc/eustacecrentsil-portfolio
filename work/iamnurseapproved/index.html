01 / The business

About Nurse Approved

Nurse Approved educates and trains nurses. Its site says it is accredited as a provider of nursing continuing professional development by the American Nurses Credentialing Center's Commission on Accreditation, and is a CA BRN approved Continuing Education Provider.

Courses run in person, as live webinars and on demand, for individuals and groups.

02 / The problem

What they needed

Nurse Approved needed one place online for its courses, free sessions and sign-ups.

03 / My role

What I did

I worked on this project through Clottware, the company I co-founded and ran from October 2023 to December 2025.

  • Requirements. I ran the first interviews with the client to work out what the site needed to do.
  • Project management. I scoped the work, set the timelines and kept the client updated as the build moved along.
  • Design and front end. I helped with the design and built parts of the front end.
  • Content setup. I set up the site's content.
  • QA testing. I tested the site before launch.
  • Support and client relations. After launch I handled ongoing support and was the client's main point of contact.
04 / The site

What the site includes

The site includes:

  • A courses page.
  • Sign-up, log-in and account pages, with "My Courses" and a cart.
  • Sign-ups for free critical thinking case study sessions.
  • Sections on education, learning activities, the webinar series and continuing education accreditation.
  • A list of organisations they have worked with.
  • A FAQ and a newsletter sign-up.

Stack

WordPress · PHP

05 / Results

After launch

The client saw more sign-ups, enquiries and sales, and spent less time on work the site now handles.
