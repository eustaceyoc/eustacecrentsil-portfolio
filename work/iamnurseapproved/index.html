01 / Overview

Background

Nurse Approved educates and trains nurses. Its site says it is accredited as a provider of nursing continuing professional development by the American Nurses Credentialing Center's Commission on Accreditation, and is a CA BRN approved Continuing Education Provider.

Courses run in person, as live webinars and on demand, for individuals and groups.

02 / The problem

What they needed

Nurse Approved had no website. Nurses had no single place to find the courses, sign up or get in touch, and running courses and certificates without a system meant manual admin for the team.

03 / Objectives

What the project had to do

  • Give nurses one place to find courses and sign up.
  • Sell and run courses online.
  • Send certificates automatically when someone passes.
  • Cut the admin the team did by hand.
04 / My role

What I did

I worked on this through Clottware, which I co-founded and ran from October 2023 to December 2025. Here's what I handled.

  • I interviewed the client to work out what the site needed to do.
  • I agreed the scope and timeline with the client and sent progress updates.
  • I helped design the site and built parts of the front end.
  • I added the site's content.
  • I tested the site before launch.
  • I handled support after launch and stayed the client's main contact.
  • I set up Tutor LMS for the courses and Certify'em to email certificates after a passed quiz.
05 / Approach

Approach and key decisions

The site had to do more than describe courses: it had to sell them, run them and prove completion. Rather than build that from scratch, I integrated two proven WordPress plugins and connected them into one flow.

  • Tutor LMS runs the courses, lessons, quizzes and student accounts.
  • Certify'em emails a certificate when someone passes a quiz, so no one has to send certificates by hand.

Using established plugins kept the build faster to deliver and easier for the team to manage after launch, while WordPress kept the rest of the site (sessions, FAQ, newsletter) easy to edit.

06 / Technologies

What it is built with

  • WordPress as the CMS.
  • PHP for theme and integration work.
  • Tutor LMS for courses, quizzes and accounts.
  • Certify'em for certificates on a passed quiz.
07 / Implementation

What the site includes

The site includes:

  • Online courses run on Tutor LMS.
  • Certificates through Certify'em, which emails a certificate when someone passes a quiz.
  • A courses page.
  • Sign-up, log-in and account pages, with "My Courses" and a cart.
  • Sign-ups for free critical thinking case study sessions.
  • Sections on education, learning activities, the webinar series and continuing education accreditation.
  • A list of organisations they have worked with.
  • A FAQ and a newsletter sign-up.
08 / Deliverables

What was handed over

  • A WordPress website with a courses section
  • Tutor LMS integration for courses, quizzes and accounts
  • Certify'em integration for automatic certificates
  • Content setup, pre-launch QA and ongoing support
09 / Outcomes

After launch

Nurses can buy and take courses online. Tutor LMS handles the courses, and Certify'em emails certificates after a passed quiz. That cut manual admin for the team. Nurse Approved also saw more sign-ups, course sales and enquiries.
